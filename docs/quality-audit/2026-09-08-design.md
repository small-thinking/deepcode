## Independent verification

- **375:** Algebraically, chunk statistics sum to `G = X.T @ X` and `b = X.T @ y`. A small independent NumPy check compared a single regularized solve against augmented least squares and confirmed that adding the penalty per shard produces a different answer. Under a sum-of-squares objective the penalty is `lambda * I`; under mean squared error it is `n * lambda * I`. This is reference-answer validation, not a fabricated executable design test.
