## Corrections and retained variants

- Corrected the revenue and local-minimum reference notes: negative revenue and singleton behavior are explicitly present in the published curated specs.
- Kept variable-duration task scheduling separate from the unit-time exercise, and immediate-referrer revenue separate from recursive top-K.
- Retained supported design/behavioral follow-ups in reference notes without expanding the concise core prompts into prescribed answers.
- Clarified attention mask polarity and API choices, preserved wider published options as follow-ups, and promoted half/bfloat16 reference computation to float32 to prevent intermediate overflow.
- Added three further direct candidate-source links during the second pass. Notion was updated and fetched back first. Frequency/date properties were not changed by this source review; links are not independent occurrence counts.
