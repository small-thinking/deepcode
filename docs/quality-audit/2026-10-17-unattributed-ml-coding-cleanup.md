# Unattributed ML Coding cleanup — 2026-10-17

Audit base: `c19897dadff5cd4554303c97bc13b41c4aea93a6`. Of 64 ML Coding exercises, 21 had an empty `companies` array; all already had technical topic tags. This audit treats the missing label as company/preparation-category metadata. The other 43 exercises are outside this cleanup.

The requested retention rule is positive provenance or explicit teaching ownership: retain a matching Notion General exercise or a documented user-created educational exercise, and mark it `General`. An adjacent topic, technical documentation, or standalone pedagogical value does not supply a company interview occurrence. Remove unsupported local exercises after checking their contracts, with particular attention to overlap.

Outcome: retain five as General and retire sixteen from the active catalog. General is a preparation category, not an employer or a frequency claim. No upstream Notion records or occurrence ledgers are changed.
