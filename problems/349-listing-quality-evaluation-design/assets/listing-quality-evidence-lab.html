Problem formulation

Quality is a construct, not a raw column

Define the entity and decision first: estimate listing-level risk at a declared time so a named intervention can improve guest experience and transaction reliability.

Say this“I will preserve the candidate outcomes as calibrated components before deriving any composite policy score.”
Whyretention ≠ review sentiment ≠ cancellation ≠ dispute/refund
Control confoundingNormalize by opportunity and keep market demand, availability, listing age, and responsibility for an event visible.
Labels + logging

Make every example reconstructable

At T0, log listing and host IDs, timestamp, feature snapshot/version, model score/version, policy/action, eligibility, and uncertainty. Join delayed outcome components only after their windows mature.

Leakage ruleIf information did not exist when the score was used, it cannot be a feature for that score.
Label caveatno review or no dispute may mean no opportunity, delayed evidence, or true negative
SplitUse forward-in-time validation and a held-out market test; deduplicate listing/stay entities across splits.
Model + offline evaluation

Earn complexity with slice evidence

Start with transparent per-component rates or calibrated rules. Add structured listing, host-history, and permitted text/image features only when a challenger improves relevant holdouts.

Metric stackDiscrimination + calibration + precision at operational capacity + label coverage + slice robustness.
Cold startnew listing: content and host priors + wide uncertainty → fallback, not “low quality”
Do not claimBetter offline AUC does not prove that an intervention based on the score improves guest outcomes.
Decision policy

Optimize the action, not the score

Choose thresholds against error costs and capacity. Guidance, verification, review, or support have different acceptable precision, recall, explanation, and rollback requirements.

Policy contractscore + uncertainty + threshold version + eligibility → reversible action or fallback
SafeguardsReason codes, review and appeal, action caps, fairness slices, and no automatic penalty for missing history.
Deep diveShow how moving a threshold trades captured risk against false-positive host burden.
Online validation + monitoring

Separate prediction validity from intervention impact

Among eligible score-triggered listings, randomize the proposed action against the current policy with stable assignment. Predeclare primary, guardrail, stop, ship, and rollback criteria.

High-level architectureOffline label-and-training loop produces versioned scores; the online policy and experiment loop turns them into reversible actions.
